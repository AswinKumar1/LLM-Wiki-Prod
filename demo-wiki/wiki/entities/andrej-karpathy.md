# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator. Former Director of AI at Tesla, former research scientist at OpenAI.

Known for making complex AI topics accessible through hands-on implementations and clear explanations.

## Relevant work

- **NanoGPT** — minimal GPT implementation in ~300 lines of PyTorch
- **MinGPT** — clean, educational GPT implementation
- **llm.c** — GPT-2 training in pure C/CUDA
- **The LLM Wiki pattern** (April 2026) — the pattern this project implements

## The LLM Wiki Gist

In April 2026, Karpathy published a GitHub Gist describing the [[llm-wiki-pattern]]. The Gist is intentionally abstract — it describes the idea, not a specific implementation — and explicitly notes it should work with any LLM agent, not just Claude Code.

Key quotes from the Gist:
- "This document is intentionally abstract. It describes the idea, not a specific implementation."
- "The exact directory structure, the schema conventions, the page formats, the tooling — all of that will depend on your domain."
